Case studies

Problems I've Solved

Three of the highest-impact initiatives I've owned end-to-end at Twin Health.

Onboarding & Activation
Self-Serve Member Onboarding Platform

Problem: Member onboarding was a fully manual, 15-day process requiring constant coordination across clinical, ops, and tech teams. Every new cohort created a bottleneck that blocked growth.

What I did: Mapped the full member journey, identified every manual touchpoint, and led engineering to build a self-serve onboarding platform with no-code automation and API integrations.

Onboarding time: 15 days → 10 minutes
3× faster go-to-market for new cohorts
Zero headcount increase to absorb growth
Called "the most significant platform unlock of the year" by leadership
Growth & Conversion
Booking Funnel Optimization

Problem: The booking funnel had no real-time visibility — the team couldn't see where members were dropping off, and conversion was leaking at multiple unidentified touchpoints.

What I did: Instrumented lifecycle event analytics across 5+ funnel touchpoints, ran structured A/B tests, and shipped targeted UX fixes across 8-week sprint cycles.

20% lift in booking conversion rate
Drop-offs identified and resolved 3× faster than before
Measurable revenue increase per cohort
Analytics framework adopted across other product areas
AI & Automation
AI-Led Workflow Automation System

Problem: Manual clinical and operational workflows were inflating COGS as the platform scaled. The cost of processing each member was climbing without a path to fix it.

What I did: Collaborated with data science and engineering to design ML-based routing and trigger logic, replacing manual processes across clinical and ops pipelines.

~15% reduction in COGS
200+ hours/month of manual work eliminated
~25% improvement in member engagement rates
Scaled without adding operational headcount
