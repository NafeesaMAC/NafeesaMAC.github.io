## About
I build products that turn hours of manual clinical operations work into minutes, using automation and AI agents.

At Twin Health, I have launched 44 partner programs (34 in the US, 10 in India), taken several products from 0-to-1 (a pre-enrolment mobile app, the India website and a partner-vendor referral ecosystem) and grown top-of-funnel leads 20% with condition-specific landing pages.

What I am known for
- Partner launches: end-to-end product requirements across landing pages, Salesforce, the clinical app and the member app, with API integrations and real-time coverage verification across payers.
- AI and automation: an AI agent that reads blood-draw PDFs and uploads the report (about Rs. 75K a month saved, 20 minutes of manual work down to zero); rule-based agents for member adherence and commitment tracking (about 12 hours down to 5 minutes per task); automated account creation from Salesforce; and incident alerts that cut response time from 24-48 hours to 30 minutes.
- Operational products: dashboards and work queues that cut onboarding time per member from 45-50 to 22-30 minutes.

How I got here
I started in public health research (MPH, Occupational and Environmental Health) and ran a randomised trial of LPG subsidies published in World Development Perspectives. I then moved through research program management and operations into product, so I understand clinical workflows, evidence and the people who use the tools.

Core strengths
Product strategy | Roadmapping | Go-to-market | AI agents and workflow automation | Stakeholder management | Product analytics (Mixpanel) | Agile | Certified Scrum Product Owner

Open to conversations about product roles in digital health and healthcare technology.
